# final-exhibit-privacy
Privacy policy and legal disclosures for the Final Exhibit mobile application.

Last Updated: February 2026

Final Exhibit is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app is built for investigators, process servers, and professionals who require secure, local documentation without cloud dependency.

⸻

1. Data Collection

Final Exhibit does not collect personal data.
	•	No user accounts
	•	No registration
	•	No analytics trackers
	•	No third-party SDKs that collect personal information

Final Exhibit does not request or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, or identifiers tied to you as a user.

⸻

2. Local-Only Storage

All photos, scans, notes, metadata, and exports created in Final Exhibit are stored locally on your device.
	•	No servers are used
	•	No cloud storage is used
	•	No background uploads occur

If the app is deleted, the device is reset, or local storage is cleared, data cannot be recovered.

⸻

3. Photos, Camera & Files

Final Exhibit may request access to:
	•	Your camera (to capture evidence photos or scans)
	•	Your photo library (to import existing images)
	•	Local files (to export PDFs or ZIP reports)

These permissions are used only on-device and solely to perform app functionality.

⸻

4. Location Data

If enabled by the user, Final Exhibit may read location metadata:
	•	From photo EXIF data
	•	From live GPS during scans

Location data is:
	•	Stored only on your device
	•	Embedded only into files you choose to export
	•	Never transmitted off-device

⸻

5. AI Assist (On-Device Processing)

Final Exhibit may offer optional AI-assisted features such as:
	•	Purpose suggestions
	•	Tag suggestions
	•	Draft notes or summaries

All AI processing occurs on the device when supported.
No photos, scans, or text are sent to external servers.

⸻

6. Sharing & Exporting

When you choose to share or export:
	•	Files are shared using the system share sheet
	•	You control where data is sent (email, files, storage, etc.)

Final Exhibit does not intercept, store, or transmit shared content.

⸻

7. Third-Party Services

Final Exhibit does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising networks, or data brokers.

⸻

8. Children’s Privacy

Final Exhibit is not intended for use by children under 13.
The app does not knowingly collect information from children.

⸻

9. Your Responsibility

Final Exhibit is a documentation tool only.
You are responsible for:
	•	Legal compliance in your jurisdiction
	•	Proper use of captured content
	•	How exported or shared data is used

⸻

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect app changes or legal requirements.
Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.

⸻
